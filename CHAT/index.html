TransportWebRTC DTLS encryption, peer-to-peer

Messages, photos, videos and calls travel directly between your two devices over WebRTC's encrypted channel. Text chat is kept only inside this browser tab so it survives a refresh, and is erased when you close the tab, leave, or change the code. Nothing is uploaded, tracked or logged. Note: this is transport encryption, not separate end-to-end app encryption. A free public signaling service is used only to introduce the two devices.
